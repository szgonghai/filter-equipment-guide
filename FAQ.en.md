# Filter Equipment FAQ

## Q1: What is the difference between bag filters and cartridge filters?

**A:**

| | Bag Filter | Cartridge Filter |
|--|-----------|----------------|
| Filtration | 1~200 μm | 0.1~100 μm |
| Flow Rate | High | Medium |
| Maintenance | Bag replacement (fast) | Cartridge replacement |
| Best For | High flow, coarse filtration | Fine filtration, high purity |

---

## Q2: How to select filter cartridge material?

**A:** Based on medium properties:

| Medium | Cartridge Material |
|--------|-------------------|
| Water / cooling water | SUS316, PP |
| Strong acids (HCl, H2SO4) | PTFE |
| Strong alkalis (NaOH) | PP, PTFE |
| Organic solvents | PTFE |
| Fluorine-containing media | PTFE |

---

## Q3: What is ISO 8573?

**A:** ISO 8573 is the international standard for compressed air quality. Key classes:

| Class | Particles (μm) | Water (pressure dew point °C) | Oil (mg/m³) |
|-------|---------------|------------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | 0.1 | -70 | 0.01 |
| 2 | 1 | -40 | 0.1 |
| 3 | 5 | -20 | 1 |
| 4 | 15 | +3 | 5 |

## Q4: Refrigerated vs. desiccant air dryer — how to choose?

**A:**

| Requirement | Dryer Type |
|-------------|-----------|
| Dew point +3°C ~ +10°C | Refrigerated |
| Dew point -20°C ~ -40°C | Desiccant |
| Dew point < -40°C | Combined or booster |

Consider: energy cost (refrigerated lower), flow rate (refrigerated better for large flows), initial cost (desiccant higher).

---

## Q5: How often should filters be replaced?

**A:** Depends on:
- Medium cleanliness
- Operating hours
- Pressure differential indicator (primary guide)
- Operating experience

General reference: Bags 1~3 months, Cartridges 3~6 months (for reference only — replace when pressure differential exceeds setpoint).

## Q6: How to pair pumps with filters?

**A:**

| Application | Pump | Filter |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Corrosive liquid filtration | Sealless magnetic pump (PTCXPUMP PW) | Liquid filter (PTFE cartridge) |
| Electronic-grade chemicals | Sealless magnetic pump (lined) | Liquid filter (PTFE) |
| Solid-laden wastewater | ARO air-operated diaphragm pump | Bag filter |
